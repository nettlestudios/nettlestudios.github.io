Sopita Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Sopita: Sopa de Letras (the "app"), published on Google Play by Nettle, an independent studio based in Colombia ("Nettle", "we" or "us"), uses, and what rights you have over it. It applies to the app and to this page. The app is in Spanish; this English version is provided for convenience and has the same content as the Spanish one.

1. Who is responsible

The party responsible for this policy and for the app is Nettle, Colombia. For any privacy question, write to nettledoingitagain@gmail.com.

2. Information the app stores on your phone

DataPurposeWhere it is storedHow long
Progress: completed levels, coins, words found and days played in the daily challengeTo continue where you left offIn the app's private storage, on your phoneUntil you delete it or uninstall the app
Settings: text size, animations, sound and vibrationSo the game looks and sounds the way you choseIn the app's private storage, on your phoneUntil you delete them or uninstall the app

This information does not identify anyone, the app does not send it anywhere, and Nettle has no access to it.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, date of birth or any other personal data.
  • The app does not access your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.
  • Nettle receives no data from the app: not your progress, not your settings, and not the data AdMob collects.
  • The app includes no analytics or crash reporting tools of our own.
  • This page uses no cookies, tracking scripts or measurement tools.

4. Device permissions

The app does not request any permission that needs your approval: Android grants every permission it declares at install time, without asking. They are:

PermissionPurpose
VIBRATEVibrate the phone when a word is found, when a selection is incorrect and when a level is finished. It can be turned off in the game's settings
INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATERequest ads and check the connection. Your progress and settings are not transmitted
AD_ID and the three ACCESS_ADSERVICES permissionsAllow Google AdMob to use the advertising ID and Android's advertising features to select and measure ads (section 7)
WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICEAdded by the Google library AdMob uses to complete background tasks. The app shows no notifications and runs no services of its own
DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSIONAdded by the AndroidX library for the app's internal use

5. Android backup

If you have Google backup turned on on your phone, Android may include the app's progress and settings in it, so you can restore them when you change phones. This backup is made by the operating system, not by the app: it is stored in your Google account, protected with encryption, and Nettle has no access to it. It is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can turn it off or delete it from your phone's backup settings.

6. Google Play

The app is downloaded and updated through Google Play. Google handles the data of its own service, such as your account and your downloads, under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Google Play gives developers aggregate statistics, such as the number of installs, and the crash reports Android sends to Google if you allowed it on your phone. This information does not allow Nettle to identify anyone.

7. Advertising: Google AdMob

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: banner ads in some levels, interstitial (full-screen) ads between levels, and rewarded video ads, which you may choose to watch in exchange for coins. To select ads, limit their frequency, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app collects the following data from your phone and sends it to Google:

DataExample
Device identifiersThe Android advertising ID and the app set ID
Approximate locationAs inferred from the IP address; not precise GPS location
Ad interactionsWhich ads were shown and which were tapped
Technical and diagnostic informationPhone model, Android version, language and crash logs from the ads SDK

Nettle does not receive this data; it only has access to aggregate reports on the number of ads shown and the revenue generated. Google processes the data under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and explains how it uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How to manage this data:

  • In Android's settings (Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone) you can reset or delete the advertising ID and turn off ad personalization.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app requests your consent through Google's form before showing personalized ads. You can change it at any time in Ajustes → Privacidad → Preferencias de anuncios (Settings → Privacy → Ad preferences).
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for all audiences.

Except as described in this section, Nettle does not sell, rent or transfer information to third parties.

8. Retention and deletion

The app's data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can do so in two ways:

  1. In Ajustes (Settings), with the Borrar el progreso (Delete progress) option. It deletes levels, coins and words; your settings are kept.
  2. By uninstalling the app. Android deletes everything the app had stored.

The Google backup is managed from your phone's backup settings, and the data AdMob collects, by resetting or deleting the advertising ID (section 7). Since Nettle does not keep any app data on a server, there is nothing to delete on our side.

9. Security

The game data is stored in the private storage Android assigns to the app, which other apps cannot access, and it is not transmitted over the network. The data AdMob collects is transmitted encrypted to Google's servers.

10. Children

Sopita is designed for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Nettle receives no personal data from anyone, including children, and ads are limited to content suitable for all audiences. If you believe a child sent us personal data by email, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, the law gives you rights over your personal data: for example, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union and the United Kingdom, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) in the United States, Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties, Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012, Argentina's Law 25,326 and Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD). These rights include accessing your data, correcting it, deleting it, objecting to or restricting its processing, taking it to another service, and filing a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

Since Nettle does not collect or keep personal data from the app, we hold no data about you to show, correct or delete: what the app stores is on your phone and fully under your control (section 8). The data AdMob collects is processed by Google, with whom the corresponding rights may be exercised under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). To exercise any right or ask a question, write to nettledoingitagain@gmail.com.

If you write to us, we will use your address and your message only to answer you, and we will delete them once the matter is resolved, unless the law requires us to keep them.

12. International transfers

Nettle does not transfer personal data to other countries, because it does not collect it. The data AdMob collects is processed by Google on servers that may be located outside your country, with the safeguards described in its privacy policy. If you email us, the message is kept in Google's email service, which may also store it outside your country.
